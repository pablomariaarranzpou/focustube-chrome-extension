Something not working?

Start with the first one — it is by far the most common, and it takes a second to check.

You turned something on and YouTube looks the same

Refresh the YouTube tab. FocusTube applies your settings to pages as they load, so a tab that was already open when you changed a setting keeps showing the old state until it reloads.

If it still looks the same after a refresh, check the Mode tab in the popup: if the mode is Off, or a Timer session is not running, or you are outside a Schedule you set, then everything on YouTube shows on purpose, whatever the Settings tab says.

A setting used to work and suddenly stopped

YouTube changes its own page structure fairly often, and a change on their side can stop one of the toggles from finding what it is meant to hide. This is the most likely cause when a single feature breaks while the rest keep working.

It is worth reporting rather than waiting — these are usually quick to fix once someone knows which part of YouTube changed. Include what you were looking at and which toggle stopped working.

Report it on GitHub

You want the normal YouTube back for a bit

You do not have to uninstall or switch anything off one by one. Open the popup, go to Mode, and choose Off. Everything on YouTube shows again, and all your settings and blocklists stay exactly as you left them for when you switch back.

What FocusTube can see

It runs only on YouTube pages, and asks for two permissions: storage, to remember your settings, and alarms, for Focus Mode timers and schedules. There is no account, nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing about your browsing is collected.

The whole extension is open source, so this is checkable rather than a promise — read the code.

Still stuck

Open an issue on GitHub and describe what you expected and what happened instead. Mentioning your browser version and whether you are on Windows, macOS or Linux usually saves a round trip.
